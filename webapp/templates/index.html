What the sheet needs

  • Name (required), or First Name and Last Name columns
  • Phone Number: any format; numbers without a country code are treated as US
  • Email
  • Birthday: with or without a year, such as 15-Mar, Mar 15, or 03/15/2002

Similar names like Mobile, E-mail, or DOB work too. Other columns are ignored.
